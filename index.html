01 — Practice

Sound
as
material

From Chile
to Barcelona

Producer and composer based in Barcelona, working from SONEN, his studio. He builds sound pieces that work as atmosphere and as structure at once — from the hypnotic techno of his EMITR project to music for image, brand and space.

A background in design and years inside the touring industry give him a sensibility that crosses sound, image and direction. He works sound as material: texture, density and time. He also builds his own instruments and verifies them — the generative engines behind the live work exist twice, in two languages, cross-checked until both produce identical output.

EMITR is his artistic project: electronic music where his own voice and the audiovisual design — a real-time particle engine — form a single piece. The principle: show the sound, not the life of the artist.

04 — Research

Interference
as material

Where the work stops being a track and becomes a question. The current line is interference: what happens in the crossing of two wavefronts, and whether an enclosure can be built out of sound alone.

R/01

LA INTERFERENCIA — working instrument

A physically honest simulator, not an animation: the visual field and the audio are the same calculation. Each source reaches the listener through its own r/c delay, so the acoustic phase difference is the same k·Δr the renderer draws. Move the listener into a node and it genuinely falls silent. Measured nodes land where theory puts them (Δr/λ = 0.5 → −24.8 dB). Open the instrument →

R/02

Verified generators

Nine generators derived from chaotic systems and integer sequences — Lorenz, logistic map, Recamán, Collatz, Thue-Morse, Euclidean rhythm, a cellular automaton, 1/f noise. Each implemented twice, in JavaScript and in Python, cross-checked until both produce byte-identical output. The verification harness is part of the work, not scaffolding around it.

R/03

Multichannel

AZSENDE premiered multichannel in May 2026. Everything known about space until then was inference from stereo — a format that cannot do it. The gap between what has been approximated and what is actually true is the thing worth measuring.

R/04

Spatial re-voicing

Every pitch has its own wavelength, and therefore its own node spacing. A chord is not one interference pattern but several nested ones. Cross the field and the chord changes voicing — in the null of the third, root and fifth remain. Harmonic motion produced by walking rather than by the arrangement.

R/05

The cube, from the inside

The enclosure from the live show reconsidered as the surface that carries the field the audience is standing inside — seen from within rather than watched from outside. An environment that explains a physical law by letting someone occupy it, rather than a visualisation placed next to a sound.

Nothing is in either source; everything is in the crossing.
